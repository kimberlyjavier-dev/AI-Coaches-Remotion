cut of the video — if your actual recording runs a bit long or short on a

to match.

reading a manual. Slight smile in the voice. Keep energy up throughout;
don't slow down for the "quieter" shots.

## Notes for recording

- Each line is its own beat/cut — a small pause between lines is fine and
  expected, don't rush to connect them into one continuous sentence.
- Line 12 ("Booked!") wants a genuine little exclamation, not flat delivery.
- Line 10 has a real question mark — let it lift at the end before the
  turn into "Request a session."
- Once you have a recording, send me the audio file(s) (either one file per
  line, or one continuous take I can split) and I'll wire it back into
  `src/Demo/shots.ts` and re-time each shot to the real clip length.
